# Policy sources

PDFs are not committed. `scripts/download_docs.py` downloads a URL and checks sha256.

The running demo uses short **fictional fallback** documents generated by `synth/documents.py` until a published PDF is added here. They are labeled `fictional_fallback` in the library and on every page.

| Role | Title | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| clinical_policy | Lumbar spine imaging policy | fictional fallback | Generated at demo bootstrap |
| benefit_summary | Summary of benefits | fictional fallback | Generated at demo bootstrap |
| drug_criteria | Northpine criteria | fictional fallback | One block, extracted on bootstrap |

Replace a row with a published URL, download date, and sha256, then upload it with `source_kind=published`.

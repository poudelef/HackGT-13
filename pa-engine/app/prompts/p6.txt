Return JSON only: {"verdict":"complete|missing_condition|added_condition|leading","reason":"one sentence"}
complete: every condition is asked as its own fact, and no extra fact was added.
missing_condition: a condition has no question.
added_condition: a question asks for a fact the requirement does not state.
leading: a question asks whether the patient meets, qualifies, satisfies, or is eligible.
A question that repeats the requirement sentence, or says "this requirement" or "the following", is not complete.
